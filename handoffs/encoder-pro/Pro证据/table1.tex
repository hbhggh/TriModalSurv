$^{\dagger}$Where the protocol routes a setting to a reference strategy, the
entry coincides with that strategy by construction: LUAD uses mean imputation
when RNA or both modalities are masked and no compensation when text is
masked, and BRCA, BLCA and LGG use no compensation when text is masked. The
LUAD routing was enabled although the LUAD validation C-index was lower with
it than without it (0.645226 versus 0.654042). The overall advantage in four
of five cohorts should therefore not be attributed to retrieval alone, and the
LUAD margin over mean imputation is only about 0.000035. Bold marks the
highest unrounded value among the three strategies within a cohort and row;
ties are all bold. The three strategies use the same 25 frozen networks; no
model was retrained and no statistical test was performed.
